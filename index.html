Clinical Notes

10s Flush vs 30s Rehab

10s Flush: Maintenance dose for healthy joints. Mechanically clears edema and metabolic waste.

30s Rehab: Required for injured or chronically achy joints. The extended tension triggers tenocytes to lay down new collagen.

The Sweep Protocol

Performing a full-body sweep is biologically superior. Compressing a tendon squeezes fluid out. Resting 2-3 minutes allows the "vacuum effect" to draw fresh arterial blood back in. Sweeping other joints provides this perfect rest interval organically.

Safety Override
