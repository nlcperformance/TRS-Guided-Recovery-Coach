Clinical Notes

10s Flush vs 30s Rehab

10s Flush: Maintenance dose for healthy joints. Mechanically clears edema and waste.

30s Rehab: Required for injured/achy joints. Extended tension triggers collagen synthesis.

The Sweep Protocol

Compressing a tendon squeezes fluid out. Resting 2-3 minutes allows the "vacuum effect" to draw fresh arterial blood back in. Sweeping other joints provides this perfect rest interval.

Safety Override

NEVER jerk into an isometric hold. Force must be smoothly ramped up over 5 seconds. Apply only 40% effort. Do not hold your breath.
